ACCOUNT RULE UPDATE

ADMIN and OPERATOR USERNAMES
- 1 to 64 printable characters.
- Letters are allowed.
- Numbers are allowed.
- Spaces are allowed inside the username.
- Symbols are allowed, for example: . _ - @ ! # $ % & + ?
- Leading/trailing spaces are trimmed.
- Line breaks/control characters are not allowed.
- Usernames are safely quoted in .env.

ADMIN and OPERATOR PASSWORDS
- Exactly 6 characters.
- Letters A-Z / a-z and numbers 0-9 only.
- No spaces or symbols.

Examples of valid passwords:
  A1b2C3
  Op1234

IMPORTANT
- The Caddy website-entry password is separate and should remain strong.
- The Settings page now allows the Admin username to be changed directly.
- Admin may also set/reset the Operator username and password.
- Existing CLOUD-PRIMARY status, LI-COR/Gmail/ntfy credentials, schedules,
  calibration values and Caddy configuration are not changed by the OVH patch.
