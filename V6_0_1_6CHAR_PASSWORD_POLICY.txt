PBF Rise Baking Burnaby Dashboard v6.0.3

PASSWORD POLICY UPDATE

Admin and Operator Settings passwords may now be set/reset using exactly 6
characters. Only ASCII letters A-Z/a-z and digits 0-9 are allowed.

Examples of valid format (examples only; do not use these exact passwords):
  A1b2C3
  Op1234

The Caddy website-entry password is NOT changed by this update. Keep the Caddy
password strong (recommended 12+ characters).

Affected files:
- server.py
- set_admin_password.py
- set_operator_password.py
- static/settings.js
- static/settings.html

Existing longer Admin/Operator passwords will continue to authenticate until
you reset them. Any NEW or CHANGED Admin/Operator password must follow the
exact 6-character letters/numbers rule.
