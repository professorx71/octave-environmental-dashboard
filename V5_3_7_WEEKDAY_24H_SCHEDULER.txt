NEW WEEKDAY 24-HOUR NOTIFICATION SCHEDULER

Plant scenario:
- Monday: alarm notifications ON 24 hours
- Tuesday: alarm notifications ON 24 hours
- Wednesday: alarm notifications ON 24 hours
- Thursday: alarm notifications ON 24 hours
- Friday: alarm notifications ON 24 hours
- Saturday: alarm notifications OFF all day
- Sunday: alarm notifications OFF all day

Settings > Admin Login now provides:
1. One-click: Apply Mon-Fri 24H to ALL Loggers.
2. Per-logger preset: Mon-Fri 24H / Sat-Sun OFF.
3. Per-day ON/OFF switches for every logger.
4. Schedule type: 24 hours on selected days OR custom ON/OFF times.

The scheduler controls alarm notifications only. Temperature and humidity readings continue to display and log during OFF days. NOT IN USE remains a master suppression setting.
