PBF Rise Baking Burnaby Dashboard v5.2.4
Logger Communication Status Fix

Why this revision was made:
The LI-COR loggers on the mini-PC were showing data ages around 7–10 minutes.
The previous dashboard classified anything older than 5 minutes as STALE and
anything older than 15 minutes as OFFLINE, which was too aggressive for the
observed reporting interval.

New communication thresholds:
- ONLINE: 0 to 15 minutes old
- STALE: more than 15 minutes up to 30 minutes old
- OFFLINE: more than 30 minutes old or no recent data

The /api/current LI-COR lookback window was also increased from 20 minutes to
40 minutes so the server can correctly distinguish STALE from OFFLINE.

Temperature alarm limits, Gmail alarms, ntfy notifications, alarm reminders,
event history, Trends, LAN access, and the Always-On mini-PC setup are unchanged.

Upgrade on the mini-PC:
1. Stop/restart the dashboard task after replacing the dashboard files.
2. Keep your existing private .env.
3. Run the updated installer if deploying this package as a fresh installation.
